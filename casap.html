I created this piece for my Visual Thinking class. The assigment was to design a identity branding for hotel.

Concept

The hotel is based on Miami, its inspired by the tropical atmosphere and vibrant culture of the this place, combined with the natural beauty and traditions of the Dominican Republic. The Hotel blends elements of beach life, warm weather and Latin Caribbean influences to create a unique and inmerseve enviroment.
